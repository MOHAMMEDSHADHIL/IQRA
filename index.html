About IQRA

Quran education, made reachable

IQRA Online Quran Academy is dedicated to making Quran education accessible through flexible, interactive and personalized online learning. Our classes cover Quran reading, Tajweed, Noorani Qaida, memorization and Islamic studies — for both children and adults, taught in a way that respects each student's pace.

Reading & Tajweed

Correct pronunciation, fluency and the rules of Tajweed, taught step by step.

Noorani Qaida & Memorization

A strong foundation for beginners, and structured guidance for those memorizing Surahs.

Islamic Studies

Everyday Islamic knowledge, values and manners for children and adults alike.
